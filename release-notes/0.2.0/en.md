**Taalcut 0.2.0**

- **Captions for your post.** Taalcut writes the caption that goes under your video and keeps it to the platform's rules, such as Instagram's hashtag limit.
- **Keep a clip from the reel.** A shot from the reference reel can stay in your video, at its place, beside your own footage.
- **Transitions from the reel.** A dissolve or fade in the reference reel is found and drawn again in your video, and your action can be timed to land on it.
- **Longer videos.** One of your clips can run longer than the reel gave it. The music plays whole bars again, so everything after stays in time.
- **On-screen text.** Text is designed and placed for each video instead of sitting in a fixed spot.
- **Covers.** A cover is designed from your video's own frames, in the shape you choose.
- **Editing.** Pick by hand the part of a clip a scene plays, see a clip's other shots from the simple timeline, choose between up to three designs for a graphic, and find the remaining tools as tiles under More.
- **Exports.** Finished videos are saved in Movies/Taalcut/Exports under their own names.
- **Fixes.** An export that stopped part-way no longer leaves the video waiting, a refused sign-in says why, and nothing waits for the charger any more.
- After updating, Taalcut looks through your library again in the background, so it can use the new notes about what each shot shows.
