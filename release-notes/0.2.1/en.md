**Taalcut 0.2.1**

- **Editing with an on-device AI.** Asking the editor to move a clip to the end, to change how long a transition lasts, or to change what a text says reaches the right tool again. In 0.2.0 a small on-device model was not offered it.
- **Longer clips.** Asking for your part to run longer now reaches the tools that do it on a small on-device model too.
