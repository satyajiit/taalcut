## Research and rationale

Research date: 8 November 2026. These choices are editorial recommendations, not a guaranteed lift in views.

- **Watch time and sends:** Adam Mosseri’s January 2025 explanation identified watch time, likes, and sends as leading ranking signals; sends were slightly more important for content reaching nonfollowers. That supports a recognisable friend/group-trip angle. [Contemporaneous reporting of the official statement](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/). The [original Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFFyRp-pINJ/) was unavailable to the research tool, so the statement was checked through the report.
- **Relevant hashtags:** Instagram announced a gradual limit of five hashtags in December 2025 and recommended fewer, targeted tags. These captions use five related tags, with no generic #viral or #fyp claims. [Report quoting the announcement](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/), [original @creators announcement](https://www.threads.com/@creators/post/DSalXGPCWM4/media). Direct Threads access was rate-limited.
- **Search context:** Instagram’s search explanation names captions and relevant keywords/hashtags among the information used to match a query. “Vietnam” appears naturally in the first line rather than as a pasted keyword block. [Original official explanation](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works), [preserved text](https://archive.ph/QjutM). The live original returned HTTP 429.
- **Retention:** Instagram’s own Reels Insights guidance recommends using average watch time to see where a hook needs improvement. [Official Reels Insights announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/04/instagram-reels-trending-audio-and-gifts-updates/).
- **Avoid invented utility:** There is no “comment for itinerary,” fake travel budget, unsupported “hidden gem,” or promise of a guide. This is a short travel montage.

The main improvement to consider beyond the caption is the opening: about five of the 12.53 seconds are black title cards before the travel footage. Showing a strong bay or lantern-boat shot in the first second is an editorial test worth considering. The supplied edit has not been changed.

Useful measures after posting: average watch time, sends/shares relative to reach, and nonfollower reach. Compare with prior posts of similar length; do not infer success from raw view count alone.
