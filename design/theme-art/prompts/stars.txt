Draw FOUR small STAR SPARKLES, laid out as a 2 by 2 grid: one sparkle centred in each quarter of the canvas, each about half the size of its quarter, with a wide empty gap between them; nothing touches another sparkle or an edge. Each is a four-pointed twinkle star with softly curved inward sides and gently rounded points, like the sparkles the reference's mascot has when it is excited, in warm pale gold (#FFD98A), with one soft shade: a slightly deeper marigold (#F9B03F) on one half of the star. Make the four slightly different from each other: top-left a classic four-point sparkle, top-right a slightly taller and thinner one, bottom-left a chubbier one, bottom-right a small one. No faces. Nothing else.
(This replaces the "one single object" rule in the style notes: four separate small sparkles in a 2 by 2 grid.)
