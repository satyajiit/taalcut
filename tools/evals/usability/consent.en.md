# Session consent

This form is a draft for counsel. It is not legal advice, and counsel has not signed off.

## Purpose of the session

This session is to see whether a creator who is new to Taalcut can install the app and make a short video. We are learning about the product. We are not grading you.

## What is recorded

We record the screen and your voice.

## What is stored

We store a JSON record of the session: times, task outcomes, ease scores, and notes. Footage and voice stay on this machine. They do not leave it unless you agree, on the card, that a copy may leave. You can decline that and still take part.

## You can stop

You can stop at any time. You do not have to give a reason. If you stop, we stop recording.

## Who to ask

Ask the moderator in the room. After the session, ask the product owner named on the card the moderator gives you. This draft does not give a company address or a statute, and it is not an approved notice.
