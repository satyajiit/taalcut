# Tasks

Glance sheet. The words to read are in `script.en.md`. Start the timer when the task is read. Stop at the success criterion or the cap, whichever comes first.

| Id | Cap | Success | May | May not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | 10 min | The app window is open | Point at the DMG | Click for them |
| T2 | 15 min | At least one clip is visible in the library | Offer the phone or the folder you provide | Import the library for them |
| T3 | 20 min | An export file exists | Repeat that it is a starter template and not a reference reel | Choose the template or export for them |
| T4 | 20 min | An export file exists | Hand them the card with the reel link | Explain the product, or explain the link |
| T5 | 10 min | The new export uses the clip they chose | Repeat that they should swap one clip and export | Choose the clip or export for them |
| T6 | 10 min | The UI language has changed and the captions use the style they picked | Repeat that they should change the language and the caption style | Change the language or the caption style for them |

After each task, read the ease question verbatim: 1 is very hard, 7 is very easy. At the end, read the three open questions verbatim.
