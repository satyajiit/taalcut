# Changelog

## 0.2.0

A video made from a reference reel follows the reel more closely. A shot from the reel can stay in the video, a
dissolve or fade in the reel is found and drawn again with the person's action timed to it, and one of the
person's clips can run longer than its place in the reel while the music plays whole bars again. On-screen text
is designed and placed for each video, a cover is designed from the video's own frames, and Taalcut writes the
caption for the post within the platform's rules.

In the editor, the part of a clip a scene plays can be picked by hand, a clip's other shots are offered from the
simple timeline, a designed graphic comes with up to three designs to choose from, and the remaining tools are
tiles under More. Exports are saved in Movies/Taalcut/Exports under their own names. Fixed: an export that
stopped part-way left its video waiting; a refused sign-in gave no reason; some work waited for the charger.
Shots are described again after the update, with notes of what happens when. See the
[release notes](release-notes/0.2.0/en.md).

## 0.1.0

The first public release. It targets Apple silicon macOS 14 or later, with English as the supported interface language.
It includes local media import, reference-led creation, editable timelines and video export; runtimes and
models install through the component manager. Users can connect their own AI accounts. No Taalcut premium
plan, managed service or bundled AI credit sale is enabled.

It ships as a drag-to-Applications disk image, signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, with
dependency security updates, third-party notices, separate terms for the demonstration footage and a
translation contribution path. See the [release notes](release-notes/0.1.0/en.md).
