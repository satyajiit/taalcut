# Taalcut trademark policy

This project policy governs permission from the Taalcut maintainers to use the project's name and artwork.
It does not claim a registered trademark, a completed clearance search, or rights beyond those the owners
hold. It does not restrict descriptive uses or other rights the law already grants.

The marks covered here are the name "Taalcut" and the logo in [`design/logo/`](design/logo/).

This policy is original. It follows the approach of the [Mozilla Trademark Guidelines](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/foundation/trademarks/policy/): the software licence and the brand are separate, a truthful reference to the project is allowed, and a fork does not get to wear the upstream name or logo. It does not copy that policy.

## The AGPL gives no trademark rights

The code is under AGPL-3.0-or-later. That licence tells you what you may do with the software. It gives you no right to the name "Taalcut" or to the logo. A right to copy, modify, or distribute the code is not a right to brand your copy as Taalcut.

## Nominative use is fine

You may use the word "Taalcut" to refer to this project, when that reference is true. "A plugin for Taalcut", "compatible with Taalcut", and "built with Taalcut" are the kind of use that is fine. Use the word to name this project, not as the name of your own product, service, or company.

## Do not imply endorsement

Do not say or suggest that the Taalcut maintainers made, sponsor, approve, or endorse your product, fork, service, or account, unless you have written permission. Do not present the name or the logo so that a reasonable person would think your thing is an official Taalcut release.

## Do not modify the logo or use it as your own icon

Do not redraw, recolour, crop, animate into a new mark, or combine the logo with other words or symbols. Do not use the Taalcut logo, or a changed version of it, as your app icon, site icon, or account image. The files in `design/logo/` are the project mark, not a stock icon.

## Forks use a different name and icon

If you ship a modified version, or any build that is not an official Taalcut release, choose a different name and a different icon. You may say in words, without the logo, that your project is based on the Taalcut code, if that is true. Say just as clearly that it is not an official release and that the maintainers have not endorsed it.

## What is not done

This policy is not evidence of trademark clearance or registration. The launch domain is taalcut.app; the first public repository is planned under the owner's `satyajiit` account. Clearance searches and any filings need their own evidence. Do not describe "Taalcut" as a registered trademark on the strength of this policy.
